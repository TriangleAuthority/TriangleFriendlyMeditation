# Deviations from the legacy mod

Every place where the port behaves differently from Friendly Meditation 2.3, with the reason, who decided and when, and a question for the original author (wghost81) where their intent is unclear. Add an entry whenever the port deviates, in the same commit.

"Owner" is this project's owner; "porter's call" means not yet confirmed by the owner.

| # | Feature | Legacy | Port | Why | Decided | Question for the author |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FM-MENU | The defaults come from `user.settings` lines the player pastes by hand (`release/user.settings.part.txt`); without them every option reads empty (0 or off) | Every option has its default in script (`FMOptionDefault`, legacy's "Default" presets, the same values); options never set get it when a save loads, and the reads fall back to it while an option is empty | No manual step for the options; same values as legacy's install. The hotkey steps stay manual (owner, 2026-11-14) | Porter's call, as in Preparations' port, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 2 | FM-RT | The hotkey pushes FM's own `Meditation` state (Geralt sits facing where he stands) and the fast-forward pushes `MeditationWaiting` | The hotkey sits Geralt down through 5.x's `Meditate()` (5.x turns him toward open space first, about 2 s to sit); the fast-forward runs inside 5.x's `Meditation` state, which only idles | Builds on 5.x's meditation instead of replacing its states, so Ghost Mode's and Preparations' hooks on it keep working; the player sees the same thing apart from the turn | Owner, 2026-11-13 (the proposed form in [port-plan.md](port-plan.md#decisions-made)) | None |
| 3 | FM-RT | No input context change while sitting | 5.x switches to an input context `Meditation`, which has no key bindings; in hotkey mode FM switches back to the previous context on the first tick, so the hotkey's release and the other FM keys reach the game | Without it, releasing the hotkey would do nothing while seated (unverified with a real key) | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 4 | FM-RT | While meditating, all actions are blocked (`BlockAllActions`): moving does nothing, only the hotkey (or a hit, combat, a scene) ends it | Moving the left stick (or WASD) stands Geralt up, as in 5.x's own meditation; Jump does not (Space is the fast-forward key, and legacy blocked jumping) | Kept from 5.x's `Meditation` state, which FM builds on ([deviation 2](deviations.md)); a natural way to stand up. Ignoring the stick as well would mean skipping 5.x's tick | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 5 | FM-CAM | With "Meditation camera" off, Geralt meditates under the plain exploration camera (legacy removed vanilla's meditation camera); with it on and "Face the player" off, the camera stays where it is when he stands up (legacy's 1.5 s return ran only with "Face the player") | Off: 5.x's own meditation camera (facing Geralt, slightly above, without the clock's sideways offset). Standing up: 5.x turns the camera back behind Geralt in every case | Builds on 5.x's `Meditation` state ([deviation 2](deviations.md)), whose camera and stand-up return are part of 5.x's meditation; legacy's return is then not needed | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 6 | FM-CAM | FM's camera in every meditation (legacy closed the clock when an hour was chosen) | FM's camera only in meditation without the clock (FM's hotkey); meditation through the clock keeps 5.x's camera, which makes room for the clock beside Geralt | The clock stays open over the world for the whole clock meditation ([deviation 8](deviations.md)); FM's camera would put Geralt behind it | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 7 | FM-FIRE | With Preparations installed, Preparations decides whether the fire may be lit (timber, interiors, settlements) and takes the timber, through FM's `fmedCanSpawnCampfire` and the timers `CampFireErrorMessageTimer`/`CampFireResourcesTimer` | FM lights its fire whenever "Spawn campfire" is on; its fire is tagged `fmed_campfire` | Temporary, until Preparations' PREP-FMED (the interface between the two blobs, [decision 1](port-plan.md#decisions-to-make)). Without Preparations, legacy behaved the same as the port | Porter's call | None |
| 8 | FM-CLOCK | Choosing an hour closes the clock; Geralt meditates in the world until the hour, then stands up | The clock stays open over the world (5.x renders the world behind it) with its hours running and its panel moved aside; at the hour Geralt stands up and the clock closes; closing the clock early (Escape) stops the meditation. FM's fast-forward, notifications, refill and campfire as in legacy; no healing at the end (5.x's own clock heals, legacy did not) | Builds on 5.x's clock instead of replacing it ([port-plan.md](port-plan.md#decisions-made)) | Owner, 2026-11-13 (the proposed form) | None |
| 9 | FM-CLOCK, FM-KEYS | A "Stop meditation" button and key (`MeditationStop`: C, pad Y) in the clock while meditating | Not ported: closing the clock (Escape, pad B) stops the meditation | 5.x's clock is open for the whole clock meditation, so its own close does the job without a new key or input context | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 10 | FM-CLOCK | The clock opens during a running meditation and shows the current and target hours | During FM's hotkey meditation the meditation key does not open the clock | 5.x's `Meditate()` on a seated Geralt asks for the clock through `OpenUI`, which FM skips in hotkey mode so the hotkey's release does not open it | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |
| 11 | FM-QUEN | Whenever the basic Quen buff is removed, its shield expires 0.1 s later | Only when the buff is removed during FM's fast-forward | FM's accelerated effects are what remove the buff before the shield's own timer; outside them the buff and the shield end together. The shield is found through the player's current Quen sign entity (legacy cached it in the effect, a private field a wrapper cannot reach), so limiting it to the fast-forward keeps a fresh cast from being cut short | Porter's call, not confirmed by the owner | None |

(An entry keeping 5.x's meditation beside FM's was withdrawn on 2026-11-13: FM replaces it, as in legacy.)
